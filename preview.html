            The problem

            Buses carry the majority of daily commuters in Sri Lanka, but there is no public real-time information system for them. Passengers standing at a halt cannot tell whether a route is running late, is already full, or has stopped for the day because of a breakdown, strike, weather, or a diverted road.

            Our solution

            Kohomada Bus? ("How's the bus?") is a lightweight, no-login web app: a route & fare finder, and live crowd-sourced status reports.

            How the fare estimate works

            Distance between your two stops → minimum Rs. 30 for the first stage (~2.6 km) → + Rs. 8 per further 1.6 km stage → round to nearest Rs. 5. Guidance estimate only.
